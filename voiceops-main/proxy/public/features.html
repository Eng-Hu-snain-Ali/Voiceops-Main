Everything you need after the call

VoiceOps goes past transcription to the work that actually follows a meeting — the tasks, the risks, the decisions, and the email.

🎙️

Speaker-diarized transcript

Speechmatics separates each speaker so you get a clean "who said what" record — not a wall of text. Custom vocabulary keeps brand names and acronyms accurate.

🗂️

Action items with owners & dates

Every commitment becomes a card with an owner and a due date, resolved from phrases like "by Thursday" against the real meeting date.

⚠️

Risk radar

Objections, blockers and competitive threats are surfaced and ranked low / medium / high, so nothing quietly derails the deal.

✅

Decisions log

The things you actually agreed on, captured with the verbatim quote that backs each one.

🌡️

Call sentiment

A quick "call temperature" read — positive, neutral, negative or conditional — from Speechmatics sentiment analysis.

✉️

One-click follow-up email

A complete, ready-to-send recap email drawn only from the agreed next steps — grounded in real quotes, never invented.
