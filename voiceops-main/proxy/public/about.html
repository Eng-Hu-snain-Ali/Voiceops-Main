Meetings should end with action, not homework

Every sales, customer-success and product team loses the same 15–20 minutes after every call: writing up notes, digging out the commitments, and drafting the follow-up email from memory. The work is important, it's repetitive, and it's exactly the kind of thing software should do.

VoiceOps closes that gap. Drop in the call audio and it returns a diarized transcript, an action board of tasks, risks and decisions, a read on the call's sentiment, and a follow-up email you can send in one click — in about a minute.

Who it's for

  • B2B sales running discovery and deal calls all day.
  • Customer success managing renewals, QBRs and check-ins.
  • Product teams and founders synthesizing user interviews.

How it's built

VoiceOps was built for the AI Factory hackathon (NativelyAI × lablab.ai) primarily in native.builder, which powers the application experience, workflows, data layer and deployment, while a small external engine handles the integrations native.builder couldn't directly provide: Speechmatics for transcription, diarization and sentiment, and an AI/ML API LLM for structured extraction of action items, risks, decisions and the follow-up email. The extraction is deliberately strict — every item is tied to a verbatim quote from the call, so VoiceOps never invents a commitment that wasn't made.

What's next

  • Push tasks straight into CRMs and trackers (HubSpot, Asana, Jira).
  • Firmographic enrichment (via Bright Data) for the companies and people named on a call.
  • A live, in-call mode that builds the board in real time.
  • Multi-language support across Speechmatics' 50+ languages.
