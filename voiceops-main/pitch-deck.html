How it works

Two live integrations, one clean flow.

🎙️ Speechmatics

Batch transcription with speaker diarization, an auto summary, and sentiment. This is the "who-said-what" backbone.

🧠 AI/ML API (LLM)

Turns the diarized transcript into structured JSON — action items, risks, decisions — and drafts the email. Strict schema, repair fallback.

🌐 Bright Data · roadmap

Planned, not yet wired: firmographic enrichment for the companies and people named on the call.

Built on native.builder   native.builder hosts the deployed app; a custom-code block calls our zero-dependency AI engine.
